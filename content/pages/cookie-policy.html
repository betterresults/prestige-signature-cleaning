Cookie policy

Which cookies this website uses and how you can control them.

Last updated: October 2026

What cookies are

Cookies are small files saved on your device when you visit a website. They help a site work properly or tell the owner how it is used.

Cookies we use

This website only uses what is needed for it to work. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts, and our quote form may be provided by our booking system. These services may receive basic technical information, such as your IP address, so the content can be delivered to your browser.

If we add analytics or marketing cookies

If we start using analytics or marketing cookies in the future, we will update this policy and ask for your consent before they are used.

Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time. Blocking essential cookies may stop parts of the website from working properly.
