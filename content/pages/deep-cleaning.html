What is a deep clean?

A deep clean is a one time, detailed clean of your whole home. Where a regular clean keeps things tidy, a deep clean deals with the build up that collects over time: grease in the kitchen, limescale in the bathroom and dust on skirting boards, doors and frames.

It is ideal before starting regular cleaning, as a seasonal refresh, or when your home simply needs more attention than usual.

Deep cleaning starts from £120, based on a minimum of 4 cleaner hours at £30 per cleaner hour. The time needed depends on the size and condition of your home and the tasks we agree. Homes with four or more bedrooms, and larger or more complex deep cleans, are assessed first with a free home consultation.

When you book, tell us about any areas that need extra attention, so we can plan the right amount of time for your home.

How is a deep clean different from a regular clean?

A deep clean goes into much more detail, dealing with built up grease, limescale and dust in places a regular clean doesn’t usually reach.

How much does a deep clean cost?

From £120, based on a minimum of 4 cleaner hours at £30 per cleaner hour. The time needed depends on the size and condition of your home, and your total is confirmed before we start.

How long does a deep clean take?

It depends on the size and condition of your home. We confirm the time with your price before you book.

Do I need to be at home?

No. Many clients give us a key or access instructions. Keys and access are agreed before the clean.

Do you bring cleaning products?

You choose: provide your own products, use ours, or choose our eco friendly range. Your price is confirmed before the first clean.

Do you visit larger homes before pricing?

Yes. Homes with four or more bedrooms get a free home consultation. Larger or more complex deep cleans and end of tenancy cleans are assessed before we confirm your price.

Can I book regular cleaning after a deep clean?

Yes. Many clients start with a deep clean and then book regular visits to keep the home at that standard.

What if something isn’t right?

Contact us as soon as possible. We will look into it and agree with you how to put it right.

Which areas do you cover?
