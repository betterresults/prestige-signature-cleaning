How we clean clinics and aesthetic practices

Every clinic is different, so we start with a walkthrough. We look at your treatment and consultation rooms, reception, patient flow, access and the standard you expect, and we listen to any concerns you have.

From there we agree a personalised cleaning checklist for your clinic. Clinic cleaning starts from £25 per hour, and your final price is confirmed once we understand your clinic and your requirements.

Where the site needs it, we use colour coded cleaning controls, so cloths and equipment are kept separate for each area to help reduce cross contamination. We only provide specialist clinical cleaning where we have the right competence and procedures, and clinical waste is handled under your own arrangements.

Cleans are planned around your opening hours and appointments, and you have one point of contact for any questions or changes.

How much does clinic cleaning cost?

Clinic cleaning starts from £25 per hour. Your final price depends on the size of your clinic, how often you need us and your requirements, and is confirmed after a walkthrough.

Do you clean cosmetic and aesthetic clinics?

Yes. We clean private, cosmetic, aesthetic and dental clinics within our competence.

What happens at the walkthrough?

We visit your clinic, look at each area, listen to your concerns and agree a personalised cleaning checklist and price.

Do you use colour coded cleaning?

Yes, where the site needs it. Cloths and equipment are kept separate for each area to help reduce cross contamination.

Do you handle clinical waste?

No. Clinical waste is handled under your own arrangements. We empty general waste bins as agreed.

Can you clean around appointments?

Yes. We plan cleaning around your opening hours and appointments.

Are your cleaners checked?

Yes. Our team is DBS checked, vetted and trained, and briefed on your clinic before the first clean.

Which areas do you cover?

We cover London, Romford, Havering, Brentwood and nearby Essex. Tell us your clinic address when you book a walkthrough.
