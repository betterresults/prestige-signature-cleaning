What is included in office cleaning?

Office cleaning keeps your workplace clean and presentable on a regular schedule, whether that is daily, a few times a week or weekly. The exact tasks depend on your office, so we start with a free walkthrough.

During the walkthrough we look at the layout, how the space is used, the busiest areas and any access or security requirements. We then agree a clear cleaning specification so you know exactly what is included.

We plan cleans around your working hours so we don’t get in the way of your team or your clients. Keys, alarm codes and access are handled through agreed procedures.

If your needs change, we review the specification with you so the arrangement keeps working for your business.
