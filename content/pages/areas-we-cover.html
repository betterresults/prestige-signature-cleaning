Questions about the areas we cover

Do you cover all of London?

Yes. We clean homes and businesses across all of London, from Central London to the outer boroughs, and in Romford, Havering, Brentwood and nearby Essex. Before we confirm any booking, we check the day, time, size of the job and how often you need us, so we only take on work we can deliver properly and on time.

Are all your services available in every London area?

Yes. Regular home cleaning, deep cleaning, end of tenancy, Airbnb, HMO, office, clinic, communal areas and post construction cleaning are all available across London and nearby Essex. For offices, clinics, communal areas and post construction cleans, we visit first for a free walkthrough so your quote matches the real job.

Is your team local to my area?

We plan every booking around where your property is. We only confirm a booking once we know we can staff it reliably in your area, so your cleaner can reach you on time, visit after visit.

How do I check if you can clean at my address?

Add your postcode when you request your quote, or send us a message on WhatsApp. We will confirm whether we can cover your address and the days and times that suit you.

Do you clean offices and flats in Central London buildings?

Yes. Many Central London buildings have their own rules for access, keys, lifts and working hours. We agree all of this with you, or with your building manager, before the first clean, so the team arrives knowing exactly what to do.

Can you clean on the days and times that suit me?

Tell us your preferred days and times when you request your quote. We will confirm what we can offer before you book. For businesses, we plan cleans around your opening hours so we don’t get in the way of your staff, clients or patients.

Do I need to be home while you clean?

No. Many clients give us a key or access instructions. Keys, alarm codes and entry details are agreed before your first clean and handled carefully, in line with the instructions you give us.

Can you look after several properties across London?

Yes. If you are a landlord, letting agent or property manager with properties in different parts of London, we set out a clear cleaning specification for each one and you deal with one point of contact for all of them.

Do you cover Romford, Brentwood and Essex?

Yes. We cover Romford, Havering, Brentwood and nearby Essex as well as London. Send us your postcode and we will confirm availability.
