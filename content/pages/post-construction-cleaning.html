What is a post construction clean?

A post construction clean, also called an after builders clean, is a detailed clean after building, renovation or fit out work. Building dust is very fine. It settles on every surface, inside cupboards and on top of doors and frames, and it often comes back if it is only wiped once.

We clean room by room from top to bottom, removing dust, leftover marks and labels from surfaces, fittings and glass, so the space is ready to use.

We clean after the building work has finished and the builders have removed their rubbish and materials. Removing building waste is not part of the clean.

Every job is different, so we look at the site first. The size of the property, the type of work and how much dust has been left all affect the time needed and your price.

When should I book a post construction clean?

Once all the building work has finished and the builders have removed their rubbish and materials.

Do you remove building waste?

No. Building waste and materials should be removed before the clean.

Do you offer a free walkthrough?

Yes. We look at the site first so the scope and price are based on the actual job.

Can you remove paint spots and labels?

We remove paint spots, marks and labels from surfaces where possible without damaging them.

Do you clean homes and workplaces?

Yes. We clean homes after renovations, and offices and shops after fit outs.

How long does it take?

It depends on the size of the property and the amount of dust. We confirm the time with your price.

Do you work with builders and developers?

Yes. We can clean finished properties ready for handover.

Which areas do you cover?

We cover London, Romford, Havering, Brentwood and nearby Essex. Tell us the property address when you request a walkthrough.
