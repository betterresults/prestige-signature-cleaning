Deep cleaning starts from £120, based on a minimum of 4 cleaner hours at £30 per cleaner hour. Yes. Our team is DBS checked, vetted and trained, and we are fully insured.

You choose: provide your own products, use ours, or choose our eco friendly range. Your price is confirmed before the first clean.

We arrange cover ourselves. You won’t be left without a clean or asked to find a replacement.

What if something isn’t right?

Contact us as soon as possible. We will look into it and agree with you how to put it right.

For offices, clinics, HMOs, communal areas and post construction cleaning we offer a free walkthrough. Homes with four or more bedrooms get a free home consultation.

How do I book?

Fill in the quote form, message us on WhatsApp or call us. We will confirm the details and your price before booking.

Can I cancel or reschedule?

Yes. Please give us as much notice as possible. Late cancellations or last minute changes may be charged, and any charge is set out in your quote.
