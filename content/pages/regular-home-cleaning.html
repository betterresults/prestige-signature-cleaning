What is regular home cleaning?

Regular home cleaning is a planned clean of your home that happens on a set schedule, usually every week, every two weeks or once a month. Instead of a big clean now and then, your home stays clean all the time.

Before the first visit we agree what you want cleaned and how often. Some clients want every room covered each time. Others want us to focus on the kitchen and bathrooms. The plan is built around your home and your routine.

Regular home cleaning starts from £66, with a minimum of 3 cleaner hours per visit. The hourly rate depends on the products you choose: £22 per cleaner hour with your products, £25 with ours, or £30 with our eco friendly range. The time needed depends on the size and condition of your home and the tasks we agree.

If your home hasn’t been cleaned professionally for a while, we may suggest starting with a deep clean, so your regular visits can keep it at that standard.

How often can you clean?

Weekly, fortnightly or monthly. We agree the schedule that suits you before the first clean.

How much does regular cleaning cost?

From £66 with your products (£22 per cleaner hour), £75 with our products (£25 per cleaner hour) or £90 with our eco friendly products (£30 per cleaner hour), based on a minimum of 3 cleaner hours. Your total, including any extras, is confirmed before the first clean.

Do I need to be at home?

No. Many clients give us a key or access instructions. Keys and access are agreed before the first clean.

Do you bring cleaning products?

You choose: provide your own products, use ours, or choose our eco friendly range. Your price is confirmed before the first clean.

What if my cleaner is off?

We arrange cover ourselves. You won’t be left without a clean or asked to find a replacement.

Can I change or skip a visit?

Yes. Please give us as much notice as possible. Late cancellations may be charged, and any charge is set out in your quote.

Do you help older people at home?

Yes. We offer home cleaning for older people. This is cleaning only, not personal care.

Which areas do you cover?

We cover London, Romford, Havering, Brentwood and nearby Essex. Add your postcode when you book and we will confirm availability.
