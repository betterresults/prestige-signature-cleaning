What is an end of tenancy clean?

An end of tenancy clean is a deep, detailed clean of the whole property when a tenancy comes to an end. It goes much further than a regular clean. We clean inside cupboards, drawers and wardrobes, degrease the kitchen, descale the bathroom and wipe every skirting board, door and switch, so the property looks the way it did when the tenancy started.

At check out, the letting agent or inventory clerk compares the property with the inventory taken at the start of the tenancy. Cleaning is one of the most common reasons for deposit deductions, so a proper clean gives tenants the best chance of getting their deposit back.

For landlords and letting agents, a professional clean means the property is ready for photos, viewings and the next tenant without delay. We work to a clear checklist, fit around your check out date, and check the work before we leave.

Every property is different. When you book, tell us the size of the property, its condition and anything the agent has asked for, and we will plan the clean around it.

How to get ready for your end of tenancy clean

A few simple steps help us do the best possible job.

  1. Move your belongings out firstAn empty property lets us reach every shelf, cupboard and corner.
  2. Empty and switch off the fridgeLet us know if you want the inside of the fridge or freezer cleaned.
  3. Check your inventoryIf the agent has highlighted anything in particular, tell us when you book.
  4. Arrange access and keysLet us know how we get in and where to leave the keys when we finish.
  5. Keep the power and water onWe need both to clean properly, including lights in every room.
  6. Book in good timeChoose a date after you have moved out and before your check out or key handover.
