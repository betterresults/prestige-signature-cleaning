If you are booking a cleaner for the first time, one of the first questions is whether you need a regular clean or a deep clean. They sound similar, but they do different jobs. Here is how to tell which one your home needs right now.

What is a regular clean?

A regular clean keeps a home that is already in good condition clean and fresh. It happens on a schedule, usually weekly, fortnightly or monthly, and covers the jobs that build up week to week.

On a regular visit our team cleans the kitchen, bathrooms, bedrooms and living areas. That means wiping and sanitising worktops, cleaning the hob and the outside of appliances, cleaning the toilet, bath, shower and sink, dusting surfaces, making beds, emptying bins, vacuuming carpets and mopping hard floors.

Because the same standard is repeated every visit, dirt never gets the chance to build up. That is what makes regular cleaning so good value over time.

What is a deep clean?

A deep clean is a detailed, top to bottom clean for when a home needs a full reset. It reaches the places a regular clean doesn’t, and deals with the grease, limescale and dust that build up slowly in corners, behind taps and on top of cupboards.

In a deep clean we go further in every room. In the kitchen we degrease worktops, splashbacks and tiles, descale the sink and taps, and clean the hob and extractor hood. In the bathroom we descale the bath, shower and screen, scrub the tiles and grout, and wipe cabinets inside and out. Around the home we wipe skirting boards, doors, frames, light switches, sockets, windowsills and radiators, and clean under and behind reachable furniture.

Five signs your home needs a deep clean first

  • It has been a while. If your home hasn’t had a thorough clean for a few months, a deep clean brings it back to a standard that regular cleaning can then keep.
  • You can see limescale or grease. Cloudy shower screens, crusty taps and sticky kitchen tiles need more time and attention than a regular visit allows.
  • You are starting regular cleaning. Starting from a properly clean home means every regular visit keeps things fresh, rather than slowly catching up.
  • The seasons are changing. A spring or autumn deep clean is a good way to refresh the whole home once or twice a year.
  • Something special is coming up. Guests, a new baby or a family event are all good reasons to get the whole home ready.

Our tip: many of our clients book one deep clean, then move on to regular cleaning. Your home gets a full reset, and the regular visits keep it that way.
